Our Craft

Every Ijemen piece begins as sketches on a workshop table, refined with the client before a single plank is cut. We source hardwoods — teak, walnut, mahogany — for grain and durability, then hand-finish every joint the traditional way, without shortcuts.

From frame to final polish, a single piece can take two to six weeks depending on complexity. That patience is why an Ijemen sofa or table is built to outlast trends — and to be handed down, not replaced.
